Hierarchical Gaussian Filter

How a subject's perceived probability tracks a changing reward world, and the prediction error that drives every update.

The update, in one line per step

Predict:   p̂t = σ(μ̂₂)  — the perceived P(reward) before seeing the outcome.

Surprise:   δ₁ = ut − p̂t  — prediction error (outcome − belief).

Learn:   μ₂ ← μ̂₂ + (1/π₂)·δ₁  — belief moves toward the outcome.

Adapt:   learning rate = 1/π₂ rises with perceived volatility (level 3).

3-level binary HGF (Mathys et al. 2014), the model fit in analysis/hgf/model.py. ω₂ is the one perceptual parameter the project actually fits; ω₃ and κ are fixed by default.

The task — the reward world both tabs see

Trials 80
Reversals (world changes) 4
Feedback (trials observed) 100%

Gamble win-probability switches between the task's levels {0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 0.8}. Below 100% feedback, unobserved trials get no outcome — the belief diffuses and uncertainty grows, exactly as on the real task's safe-choice trials.
